Zaur Kapanadze

Hello! I am currently a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at Texas Tech University, majoring in Comparative Politics and American Politics with a minor in Methodology.

My dissertation explores how oligarchs influence elections and policy outcomes in the Republic of Georgia. Existing scholarship has only focused on short periods of time and formed preliminary hypotheses whose empirical examination has not always been systematic. Using a mixed-methods approach, I demonstrate what influence oligarchs have on elections and how they shape different policy outcomes. For my dissertation, I have received a Dissertation Research Grant from the most prominent area studies association for post-Communist scholars, the Association for Slavic, East European, and Eurasian Studies (ASEEES).
