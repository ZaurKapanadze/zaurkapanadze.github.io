Hello! I am currently a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at Texas Tech Univeristy, majoring in Comparative Politics and American Politics with a minor in Methodology.

My dissertation explores how oligarchs infleunce elections and policy outcomes in the Republic of Georgia. Exisiting scholarship has only focused on short periods of time and formed preliminary hypotheses whose empirical examination has not always been systemic. Using a mixed-methods approach I demonstrate what infleunce oligarchs have on elections, and how they shape different policy outcomes. For my dissertation I have received a Dissertation Research Grant from the most prominant area studies association for post-Communist scholars, Association for Slavic, East European, and Eurasian Studies (ASEEES).
